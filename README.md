**Superstore Sales Dashboard (Power BI)**

The objective is to transform raw retail data into a comprehensive, interactive, and visually engaging executive dashboard built in Power BI.   
The dashboard provides deep visibility into top-level key performance indicators (KPIs), temporal trends, category breakdowns, regional contributions, shipping performance, and product-level profitability leakages.

**Dataset:** Superstore Sales Dataset
**Total Transactions:** 9,994 rows
**Key Fields Visualized:** Order Date, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount, Category, Sub-Category, Region, Ship Mode, and Customer Segment.

**Dashboard Visual Layout & Components**

**1. Top KPI Summary Cards**
**Total Orders:** 5,009 transactions tracked across the network.
**Total Sales:** $2.30M in overall revenue.
**Total Profit:** $286.40K in net earnings.
**Profit % :** Overall company profit margin of 12%.
**Global Slicers:** Interactive dropdown filters for Category and Region, plus a dynamic Date Range Slider (03-01-2014 to 30-12-2017).

**2. Temporal & Regional Trends (Left Section)**
**Total Sales and Total Profit by Year:** Dual-metric trend line mapping continuous upward growth from 2014 through 2017, highlighting steady multi-year scalability.
**Total Sales and Profit% by Region:** Column-and-line combination chart demonstrating that the West and East lead in sales volume, while margins fluctuate across territories (Central recording a lower margin of ~7.9%).
**Total Sales by Category and Sub-Category:** A detailed hierarchical treemap showcasing revenue contributions across Technology (Phones, Accessories, Copiers), Furniture (Chairs, Tables, Bookcases), and Office Supplies.

**3. Category Breakdown & Deep-Dives (Center Section)**
**Total Profit by Category:** Waterfall/Column breakdown revealing net profit contributions per core category—highlighting Office Supplies and Technology as major profit drivers.
**Category, Sales and Profit Scatter Plot:** Individual product-level correlation plot contrasting Sales against Profit, colored by Category to isolate high-value outliers and loss-making items.

**4. Logistics, Sub-Categories & Shipping (Right Section)**
**Profit Percentage by Ship Mode:** Bar chart showing consistent return margins across shipping methods (First Class yielding ~13.9%).
**Total Profit by Sub-Category:** Horizontal bar chart highlighting top profit makers (Copiers, Phones, Accessories) versus bottom underperformers running into negative returns.
**Total Sales by Ship Mode:** Donut chart illustrating fulfillment distribution, showing that Standard Class dominates the majority of shipping volume at 59.12% ($1.36M).

**How to View / Run This Project**
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the .pbix file using Power BI Desktop.
3. Utilize the top slicers and date range slider to interactively cross-filter the entire report canvas.
